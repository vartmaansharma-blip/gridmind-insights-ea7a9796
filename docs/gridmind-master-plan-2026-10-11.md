## 4. First-product approval requirements

Evaluate each candidate against all fifteen questions; record unknowns rather than filling them with model scores:

1. Specific customer and actual purchasing authority.
2. Direct evidence of the problem.
3. Frequency and recurrence.
4. Verified or defensibly estimated financial consequences.
5. Actual root cause, separately from suspected causes.
6. Whether the relevant cause is controllable.
7. Availability and reliability of operational records.
8. Current manual and software alternatives.
9. Why those alternatives fail or are inadequate in the actual case.
10. GridMind's expected and then observed incremental value.
11. Engineering and implementation requirements.
12. Actual willingness to pay and purchasing next step.
13. Repeatability across manufacturers.
14. Potential for international expansion, without expanding the first test.
15. Evidence that would invalidate the opportunity.

Weighted evaluations are optional reasoning aids; weights/scores are assumptions, never verified customer facts. The first approved product must solve a narrow, recurring, economically meaningful problem. A single anecdote, synthetic comparison or speculative price cannot pass this gate. The tracker retains small-sample learning thresholds; these are internal operating gates, not statistical validation.

## 5. Shared intelligence model and approval boundaries

**Evidence → Investigation → Qualified Human Review → Approved Action → Outcome Verification → Institutional Learning.**

Preserve original records and source-linked permissioned provenance; record missing/contradictory data explicitly. Separate observations, hypotheses, suspected causes, verified engineering conclusions and economic estimates. Qualified domain review and customer approval are distinct. No automatic physical-process modification. Verify outcomes with a defensible baseline, comparability and confounders; do not claim savings from a completed action or descriptive difference.

Customer authorization, privacy, tenant isolation, custody/retention, reviewer competence and applicable security/database release gates precede real records. Reuse learning only where data rights and evidence quality permit. Preserve existing organization/factory/asset/case/Auth/revision primitives, TanStack/React/Vite/Bun, Lovable configuration, electricity functionality and green **#9ADD00**. No new app or duplicate master plan.
