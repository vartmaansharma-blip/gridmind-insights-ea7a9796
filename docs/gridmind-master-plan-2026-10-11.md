## 1. Ambition and current commercial decision

GridMind aims to become a globally relevant **industrial and critical-infrastructure intelligence company**, serving India and eventually international markets. The founder's latest direction ([G31-C20-09](gridmind-decision-constitution-and-contradiction-register-2026-10-10.md#manufacturing-intelligence-direction-20-october-2026)) frames this as a **progressively autonomous industrial intelligence operating system**, earned one demonstrated capability and permission boundary at a time. Help organizations investigate consequential operational problems, understand dependencies, make defensible decisions, and improve the reliability and economics of physical systems. Prove one valuable product before expanding; each expansion needs separate demand, capability, safety and economic evidence.

**GridMind has not yet identified or validated its number-one commercial business opportunity.** The earlier Manufacturing Quality-Loss Intelligence ranking was premature and is **withdrawn as an approved commercial decision**. Credible manufacturing problems and working software did not establish that our proposed tool addresses the binding cause better than existing alternatives. Finishing/quality is a candidate; neither the provisional finishing-first wording in #156/#158/#160/#162 nor earlier invoice-first/R3-first priorities approve a commercial winner.

**Current starting hypothesis:** production feasibility, bottleneck investigation and delivery-risk management for Indian component manufacturers, initially learning in Jamshedpur–Adityapur. Ask “Can this order be completed to the required quality and date under the actual material, route, tooling, people, capacity and approval constraints; what evidence or decision is missing?” Prioritization follows the founder's direction, not new demand evidence. The initial product contract is [architecture §11](product/gridmind-3-1-manufacturer-resolution-system-and-architecture-2026-10-17.md#11-india-first-manufacturing-intelligence-requirements-20-october-2026); the [dated China/India evidence register](research/gridmind-industrial-intelligence-competitor-learnings-2026-10-18.md#china-india-and-incumbent-evidence-register-20-october-2026) supports the comparison without proving the local gap.

**Selection principle:** start with an actual consequential problem, identify its causes and current alternatives, prove that GridMind can materially improve an outcome, and only then approve the product. Existing technology must not determine the problem. A functioning prototype does not prove product-market fit.

No complete ERP/MES/QMS, autonomous engineering diagnosis, guaranteed savings, automatic equipment intervention or simultaneous multi-industry platform is authorized. The shared investigation architecture is a foundation, not a demonstrated moat or proof of purchasing demand.

## 2. Initial research market and observed evidence

**B2B industrial metal-component contract manufacturing** is the initial research market. Narrow by process and problem structure, independently of geography:

1. Sheet-metal stamping, pressing and fabrication.
2. Precision machining and CNC component manufacturing.
3. Metal casting and forging.

Investigate each segment separately before assuming a common product. This scope does not establish the interviewed factory's exact process. India is the first market; Jamshedpur–Adityapur is the first access/learning context under G31-C20-09. Geography is not proof of a common problem or a permanent market boundary. Plastics, electronics, textiles and food processing remain future possibilities, not simultaneous initial targets.

The [sole buyer tracker](gridmind-buyer-discovery-sprint-2026-10-11.md#interview-1-founder-reported-qualitative-account) preserves **one founder-reported qualitative manufacturer interview**: approximately 80 workers/20 machines, reported utilization near 50%, grinding/finishing labor, coordination/night-shift difficulties, approximately 15-day unavailable payment, temporary specialized-steel shortage and escalations, approximately ₹40 lakh threatened/delayed/potentially missed production or sales, Excel use and resistance to ten new sensors per machine. Date, permissions, original records, process, causes and shortage resolution remain unconfirmed. Willingness to pay depends on the exact problem and solution; no paid commitment is established.

These are reported observations, not representative industry validation: necessary grinding may be required work; 50% utilization does not identify a cause; ₹40 lakh is not verified permanent loss or lost profit; the payment account does not prove unlawful delay, intentional withholding or a documentary exception; export-prioritization allegations are unverified. Escalation does not show that GridMind could have prevented the shortage.

## 4. First-product approval requirements

Evaluate each candidate against all fifteen questions; record unknowns rather than filling them with model scores:

1. Specific customer and actual purchasing authority.
2. Direct evidence of the problem.
3. Frequency and recurrence.
4. Verified or defensibly estimated financial consequences.
5. Actual root cause, separately from suspected causes.
6. Whether the relevant cause is controllable.
7. Availability and reliability of operational records.
8. Current manual and software alternatives.
9. Why those alternatives fail or are inadequate in the actual case.
10. GridMind's expected and then observed incremental value.
11. Engineering and implementation requirements.
12. Actual willingness to pay and purchasing next step.
13. Repeatability across manufacturers.
14. Potential for international expansion, without expanding the first test.
15. Evidence that would invalidate the opportunity.

Weighted evaluations are optional reasoning aids; weights/scores are assumptions, never verified customer facts. The first approved product must solve a narrow, recurring, economically meaningful problem. A single anecdote, synthetic comparison or speculative price cannot pass this gate. The tracker retains small-sample learning thresholds; these are internal operating gates, not statistical validation.

## 5. Shared intelligence model and approval boundaries

**Evidence → Investigation → Qualified Human Review → Approved Action → Outcome Verification → Institutional Learning.**

Preserve original records and source-linked permissioned provenance; record missing/contradictory data explicitly. Separate observations, hypotheses, suspected causes, verified engineering conclusions and economic estimates. Qualified domain review and customer approval are distinct. No automatic physical-process modification. Verify outcomes with a defensible baseline, comparability and confounders; do not claim savings from a completed action or descriptive difference.

Customer authorization, privacy, tenant isolation, custody/retention, reviewer competence and applicable security/database release gates precede real records. Reuse learning only where data rights and evidence quality permit. Preserve existing organization/factory/asset/case/Auth/revision primitives, TanStack/React/Vite/Bun, Lovable configuration, electricity functionality and green **#9ADD00**. No new app or duplicate master plan.
