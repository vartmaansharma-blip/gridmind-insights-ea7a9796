## 1. One ambition and the decision we are taking

**Build GridMind from a narrowly useful, evidence-backed industrial investigation and resolution system into an industrial intelligence company, then expand into physical-infrastructure intelligence only when capability, customer value and safe delivery have been demonstrated.**

**Why:** industrial teams may struggle to turn conflicting operational evidence into a defensible decision, an owned action and a verified result. That unmet need is a **buyer/problem hypothesis**, not an established market finding. Our mission is to help them complete that loop where we can prove an improvement over their current method.

**Current product thesis:** one bounded investigation: question → permissioned sources → validated observations and competing hypotheses → qualified independent review → customer-approved action → follow-up evidence → verified or explicitly inconclusive outcome → retained learning where rights permit. The investigation case is the central object; machines, bills, quality records and supplier documents are supporting evidence when relevant.

**Decision:** finish and evaluate this connected workflow using compressed air as the initial technical exemplar. Preserve useful electricity work. Select one first commercial offer through actual buyer/problem/alternative and delivery evidence. Software maturity does not select the buyer, establish professional competence or prove willingness to pay.

**Long-term destination:** reuse earned investigation methods across industrial operations, then larger physical infrastructure. The ambition includes eventually anticipating consequential problems, but prediction must earn prospective evaluation rather than follow from historical reports. Each expansion requires a new buyer, method, rights, safety and economics decision. Grid/quality/energy/billing are not simultaneous product commitments. “Industrial Profit & Resolution Intelligence” remains a historical positioning hypothesis; GridMind and signature green **#9ADD00** remain approved identity.
