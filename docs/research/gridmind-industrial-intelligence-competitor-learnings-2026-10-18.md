## Decision in one paragraph

GridMind should learn **provenance-first document intelligence** (CADDi), **a lighthouse case leading to guided actions and measured outcomes** (Arch), **cross-functional context as a shared case model** (Noetive), **interoperability with incumbent industrial tools** (Schneider/PTC), **explicit physical-model boundaries** (Siemens/NVIDIA), **physics and topology separation** (Neara/Zepben) and **operator decision authority** (GE Vernova). **Do not build** those firms' product portfolios, sensors, autonomous agents, grid dispatch or digital twins. The first business experiment remains **R3 electricity billing/contract exposure first for discovery, R2 acceptance/invoice disputes as challenger**, R1 quality a synthetic illustration; commercial winner **unselected**. Recent industry investment is competitor/context evidence, never a buyer signal.

## Priority and 30-day interpretation

**P0 NOW:** professional energy/tariff candidate; genuine manufacturer owner conversation; **R3 synthetic tariff-screen protocol** and specialist review; **R2 interview parity**. A permitted customer bill must **not** go into public GitHub, synthetic fixtures or unsecured third-party AI. Existing owner relative introduction is an opportunity, not a completed interview, and any permission/authority remains to be established.

**P1 AFTER VALIDATION:** fixed-fee bounded review, expert-qualified finding, evidence/provenance acceptance, cash-and-cost accounting; only then paid-scope feasibility and appropriately authorized real data.

**P2 AFTER REPEAT DEMAND:** read-only systems adapters, dashboards, AI investigation assistance and multi-site expansion.

**P3 LONG-TERM:** digital twins, sitewide industrial AI, electrical network modeling and infrastructure simulation. **No present implementation or marketing claim.**
