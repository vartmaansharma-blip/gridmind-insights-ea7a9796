## What to show

1. **Records:** 100 fictional components at revision R2, BOM 1 kg per component, released lot 200 kg with 50 kg reserved, hence 150 kg unreserved; one press shift records 360 minutes against a 120-minute bounded requirement. These are invented inputs, not live factory records.
2. **Checks:** existing deterministic source, approved-producer, revision, material, capacity, dependency and safety checks. Inspect requirement, version and source beside each result. The source review is explicitly simulated.
3. **Decision:** one supplied alternative meets the sample checks; the unapproved producer is blocked. Eligibility still requires human review.
4. **Approval/action/outcome:** no real approval or transaction occurs; outcome is unverified and no savings are claimed. First agree the actual customer decision, current process, data rights, responsible reviewers and a narrow measurement/comparison plan.
5. **Export:** download the packet or inspect its input structure and hashes. It contains schema version, synthetic origin, scenario/story, historical period, original source and normalized input digests, operating summary, readable checks, strict input, exact computed decision, opportunity/economics where relevant and workflow state.

| Negative sample     | Expected behavior                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Surplus opportunity | Missing verified demand and supplier qualification prevents a trade recommendation. Missing retain value prevents an economic recommendation. Bilateral sharing is simulated. |
| Press downtime      | Recorded calendar minutes do not establish usable capacity during the outage. Trace affected operations/products/orders/work orders and block unsupported action.             |
