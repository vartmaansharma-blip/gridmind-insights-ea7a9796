## Interview questions

Ask for one recent, real event and follow its timeline. Avoid leading questions such as “Would AI help?” or “Would you buy GridMind?”

1. Tell me about the last time a production, maintenance, quality, energy, or process issue needed investigation.
2. What triggered it, and what happened next?
3. Who became responsible for resolving it, and who had authority to decide?
4. How often has a similar issue happened over the last quarter? What records would confirm frequency?
5. What was the operational and economic consequence? Separate measured facts from estimates.
6. How did the team investigate it step by step? Which systems, spreadsheets, reports, vendors, or experts were involved?
7. What remained unresolved, delayed, or uncertain after the existing process?
8. What was tried before, and why was it not sufficient? If the existing process worked well, record that.
9. Which evidence would be needed to investigate the event responsibly, and who is authorized to approve access?
10. Who owns the outcome and budget? What approval process would a small experiment require?
11. What would make an additional tool or service not worth adopting?
12. If the problem appears material, what is the smallest safe next step—and who would need to approve it?

## Decision after the conversation

Do not select a product wedge from enthusiasm or one anecdote alone.

- [ ] **Advance to a second discovery conversation:** specific incident, credible consequence, known workflow owner, and a clear gap in the current process.
- [ ] **Investigate economics:** the consequence is material enough to justify quantification; distinguish gross impact from recoverable value.
- [ ] **Investigate data access:** an authorized owner can describe a lawful, bounded evidence path.
- [ ] **Design a retrospective test:** only after written scope, permissions, and safe data handling are approved.
- [ ] **Reject / deprioritize this candidate:** existing tools solve it adequately, consequence is immaterial, data is inaccessible, no accountable owner exists, or GridMind cannot influence the outcome.
- [ ] **Access blocker:** no qualified professional or legitimate introduction route is currently available.

### Candidate status

**Not selected.** One interview can change confidence, not establish product-market fit. Require multiple independent conversations and at least one concrete buyer-approved next step before committing to a commercial wedge.
