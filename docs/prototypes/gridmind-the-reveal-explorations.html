Three different ways to make industrial complexity become understandable. Same ambition. Three distinct visual signatures. No concept is approved yet.

ILLUSTRATIVE SCENARIOS · SYNTHETIC DATA · NOT PRODUCT OUTPUT

Three executions of “The Reveal”

Use the controls in each concept to inspect the proposed transformation. These are direction tests, not a finished website or claims about current GridMind capabilities.

01 / TEMPORAL

The Context Snap

A measurement changes meaning when time aligns.

Signature

A quiet time series stays fixed while a precisely aligned event band appears. The snap into alignment is the recognisable moment.

Best at

Explaining why isolated metrics need operating context.

Boundary: temporal overlap is not proof of causation. Example data only.

02 / SPATIAL

The Layer Cut

A system opens to expose the relevant layer.

Signature

A custom industrial drawing separates into disciplined layers; the selected layer becomes legible without losing the whole system.

Best at

Communicating connected systems and helping visitors orient themselves.

Boundary: conceptual schematic only. Do not imply GridMind has plant-level integrations or a digital twin.

03 / EVIDENCE

The Evidence Fold

A proposed action opens into what supports it.

Signature

A large decision statement folds open into evidence, assumptions and gaps. The reveal is a visual challenge to unsupported certainty.

Best at

Building trust by making uncertainty and next-step evidence explicit.

Boundary: illustrative reasoning framework only; not an automated recommendation or verified result.

Memorable is not the same as complicated.

Provisional hypothesis: The Context Snap has the clearest fast-read and the most repeatable motion signature; The Layer Cut may be the most ownable visually but risks suggesting a digital twin; The Evidence Fold makes GridMind’s evidence discipline explicit but may read as a framework rather than a distinctive brand.

Do not choose a winner by taste alone. Compare recall, comprehension, industrial credibility, accessibility and relevance to the first validated commercial offer. The offer is still undecided.
