# GridMind — First Paid Case Scope Schedule (Draft Template)

**Status:** Supporting commercial operations template, 17 October 2026. No paid case, customer contract, reviewer appointment or real-data intake is established by this document. It is a checklist to complete with an appropriate adult contracting representative, qualified domain reviewer and legal adviser before presenting an actual agreement. The [Master Plan](../gridmind-master-plan-2026-10-11.md) remains the sole strategy and the [Buyer Discovery Sprint](../gridmind-buyer-discovery-sprint-2026-10-11.md) remains the sole live evidence tracker.

## 2. The problem and the buyer's decision

Complete in plain language:
- Trigger and date: [one actual event; source and observed/estimated status].
- Existing process and provider: [what the customer's own team, ERP/QMS/accountant/consultant already does well].
- Residual gap: [specific unanswered question, avoid pitching a generic platform].
- Decision at the end: [which customer role will choose among which realistic options].
- Included factory, products/orders/assets, counterparties and period: [bounded identifiers].
- Explicit exclusions: [machinery operation, certification, debt collection, litigation, financial custody, guaranteed saving or recovery, unrelated sites].
- Why external help may be worth its full cost: [buyer-stated consequence and price evidence; do not assert ROI].

If the question can be answered adequately through the customer's existing process, recommend that path. Never begin an open-ended “find every problem” engagement.

## 3. Domain-specific method schedule — choose one lane

| Lane | Minimum source questions | Qualified review | Out-of-scope claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R1 quality with linked production incident** | Drawing/specification revision, lot and process genealogy, inspection method and measurement validity, rejection/containment notice, 8D/CAPA history, production chronology, acceptance and follow-up | Manufacturing-quality specialist competent for the precise process and customer standard; record conflicts and corrective-action effectiveness method | “Root cause proven,” compliance certification or process change without adequate evidence and customer/site authority |
| **R2 acceptance/invoice dispute coordination** | Contract/order terms, delivery and receipt proof, acceptance/rejection, invoice and debit-note identities, correspondence, relevant dates, owner of each counterparty assertion | Commercial/accounting specialist; counsel when rights or legal interpretation are implicated | Debt adjudication, collection guarantee, lending, payment custody or legal opinion by unqualified staff |
| **R3 electricity exposure** | Applicable tariff and effective date, bills, meter period/units, contracted demand and terms, load/production context where relevant, customer alternatives | Electricity/tariff specialist with scope-specific competence | Physical energy audit, safety certification, grid intervention or guaranteed bill reduction |

For all lanes, define deterministic checks, assumptions, counterevidence and “insufficient evidence” conditions before analysis. R1/R2 methods are proposed specifications; the connected code currently implements only synthetic C1/C4 methods. Selecting a lane does not activate a production engine.

## 4. Evidence and custody schedule

Create a source register *before* transfer. For each proposed source, record owner, authority to disclose, original location, scope/purpose, sensitivity, allowed recipients, collection method, time period, version/hash, retention and deletion instruction. The source owner must approve rights to any onward sharing; a buyer cannot grant a supplier's rights by implication. Customer records must use an approved secure channel and least-privilege roles after the technical gate. Do not use research email, public forms, demo upload, browser storage or a model vendor as an unapproved intake route.

The customer may withhold records. GridMind may then narrow the question, return “insufficient evidence,” or stop. No missing value becomes a zero or a fact. Identify whether audio, photographs, personal data, trade secrets or site access are necessary; each needs separate approval. No AI training or cross-customer reuse is assumed. Retention, export, correction, deletion and incident-notice terms require qualified legal/security review.

## 5. Deliverables and release test

1. **Scope confirmation:** one question, source rights, method, exclusions, milestone and named owner.
2. **Source register and chronology:** source ID/version, author/owner, date/time zone, units, conflicts and unverified participant statements.
3. **Working analysis:** reproducible calculations, alternative explanations, evidence gaps, limitations and domain-specific reviewer notes.
4. **Decision brief:** answer or explicit insufficiency; fact/estimate/hypothesis labels; cited evidence; alternatives with tradeoffs; named reviewer decision bound to exact revision; customer-approved publication.
5. **Action register:** customer-chosen owner, due date, dependencies and safety/approval conditions. GridMind does not execute industrial controls or financial transactions.
6. **Effectiveness plan and follow-up:** agreed baseline, observation window, measurement owner, possible confounders, accepted/declined actions and corrected brief if new evidence changes the answer.
7. **Commercial closure:** customer acceptance or written defect list, correction window, actual specialist/founder hours, invoice and collected cash recorded separately.

A brief is rejected if it cites an inaccessible source, hides contradiction, overstates certainty, lacks required reviewer competence, uses a stale revision, exposes another tenant, or implies a verified financial outcome without a defensible baseline.

## 6. Fee, change, complaint and stop schedule

Insert a bounded fee and expense cap only after fully loaded case-cost review. Identify which work is fixed price, what triggers a change request, who can authorize it, payment milestones, cancellation and correction terms. No success fee is assumed; any such model requires separate attribution and legal review. Never count uncollected invoices as cash.

The customer retains responsibility for site safety, operating decisions and counterparty communications. The reviewer may halt unsupported technical publication. GridMind may pause for missing consent, unsafe request, conflict of interest, breach, unavailable qualified review or material scope change. Define the permitted recipients of a corrected/withdrawn brief and the complaint contact in the signed agreement. The incident procedures in the [Economics and Risk Register](gridmind-company-economics-and-risk-register-2026-10-17.md) and [Release Gate](../release-gate.md) govern response; this template alone is not an incident plan.
