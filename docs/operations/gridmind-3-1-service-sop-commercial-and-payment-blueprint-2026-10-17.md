## 1. The commercial proposition and who pays

**GridMind does not sell “AI intelligence” in the abstract.** A manufacturer can buy a bounded, expert-assisted decision record when a costly operational/commercial exception spans incompatible sources, fragmented responsibility or unclear review, and current people/ERP/QMS/accountants have not resolved it economically.

| Case lane (discovery-only) | Likely economic buyer to interview | Existing alternatives to compare | Example value deliverable | Specialist dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R1 / C2 quality with related C1 downtime** | owner, plant head, quality/commercial head | internal quality team, established QMS/ERP, 8D/quality consultants | source-typed repeat-defect, action and recurrence evidence pack; review-ready brief | qualified quality/metrology/process professional |
| **R2 / C6 acceptance/invoice dispute** | owner, CFO, commercial/accounts head | ERP/accounting, finance and customer account teams, commercial counsel | reconcile delivery/acceptance/credit/debit-note evidence, locate missing approval and decision owner | relevant commercial/tax/legal expertise if needed |
| **R3 / C4 electricity bill and contract exposure** | owner, finance/plant energy head | utility customer service, accountants, energy auditors/electrical contractors | period-to-period bill/contract anomalies and reviewer-verified questions | tariff/electrical professional; no physical audit claim |

**Do not convert entire disputed invoice face value, whole-machine output or energy bill amount into recoverable GridMind value.** Track incremental customer benefit net of current alternative, expense, attributable causation and verified realized benefit separately.

## 3. Service SOP from prospect to final economic review

| Step | Required operational action | Evidence/output | Gate & stop condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| S01 – Contact | Accept only non-confidential inquiry from an actual authorized adult/organization | consent timestamp, return route, case category, no sensitive upload | No contact recipient / notice → do not publish functioning lead form |
| S02 – Fit | Ask for one **recent** concrete costly exception, existing provider/process, actual buyer and gap | verbal one-page map; classify D/E/P | Cannot describe problem/decision owner → no generic solution pitch |
| S03 – Scope | Define one question, service/exclusions, deliverables, effort cap, assumptions and specialist cost | versioned scope/quotation | Buyer won't authorize scope/expert → decline |
| S04 – Contract | Confirm contracting entity, adult/authorized signatory, privacy/confidentiality, refund/acceptance, permitted purpose, data rights | signed scope, purchase order where required | Missing legal authority/rights → no billing or data intake |
| S05 – Commercial start | Issue valid invoice/payment request from approved business rails and record agreed advance/terms | invoice ID, payment term, bank/provider reference | Money to personal/unapproved account or false payment status → stop |
| S06 – Evidence authorization | Identify owner, documents, supplier/customer rights, purpose, retention, narrow access | consent manifest & evidence request | Third-party rights/tenant controls unresolved → no file upload |
| S07 – Intake & traceability | Quarantine permitted material, checksum, classify provenance/time/unit/source uncertainty | immutable source manifest, rejects/log | Malformed or unauthorized source → reject/ask for clarification |
| S08 – Investigation | Build observations, contrary evidence and competing explanations; no unsupported causality | auditable finding/proposed next discriminating test | Insufficient evidence → clearly inconclusive/referral |
| S09 – Qualified review | Assign appropriate independent professional and freeze revision for approval or rejection | bounded signed review record and dissent | No competence/independence → no professional conclusion |
| S10 – Customer decision | Release approved brief only to authorized customer; obtain acknowledgment and any approved action | decision log, action owner/deadline | Customer must own production, safety, purchasing and financial action |
| S11 – Effectiveness | Collect authorized follow-up/baseline with confounders and time window | observed, estimated, unverified or inconclusive outcome | No valid control/comparison → no savings/ROI claim |
| S12 – Final invoice | Reconcile contracted amount, payment events, settlement and credits; follow agreed lawful collection protocol | accounting-close and receivable ledger | Do not mistake a quote, issued invoice or gateway callback for collected cash |
| S13 – Case economics | Track expert hours/fees, internal labor, travel, overhead allocation, rework, collection costs | contribution record; prospect future repeat need | Structurally negative margin → redesign or stop |
| S14 – Learning | Retain de-identified process learnings only where customer rights permit; destroy/return records as contracted | retention/deletion log, corrected playbook | No data reuse merely because software can copy it |

**Initial delivery model is on-site-capable and human-reviewed, not online-only SaaS.** Physical factory visit requires customer authorization, qualified accompanying adult/professional, and plant safety permission; no direct machinery operation or hazardous inspection by GridMind software/founder.

## 4. Two payment rails must never be conflated

**Rail A — GridMind receives fees from manufacturers.** Revenue collection for its own documented services, to its legally authorized business bank account or regulated payment aggregator merchant account.

**Rail B — manufacturers are paid by their downstream buyers.** This is *their* existing commercial receivable. Under R2 GridMind may organize evidence and advise on next documentation steps only with permissions; it must **never take custody**, intermediate settlements, assume licensed collection/factoring/lending status or advertise guaranteed payment. RBI's Trade Receivables Discounting System is a separate regulated financing channel; GridMind is not a TReDS operator.

## 5. Payment acceptance: deliberately low-complexity phases

### Payment Phase P0 — before legal merchant readiness (CURRENT)

- **No live checkout/payment collection inside GridMind.** Public CTA is non-confidential research conversation only, not order/pay.
- A properly authorized adult/business representative with professional legal/tax advice must decide which lawful contracting entity, signatories, business bank, GST applicability, invoice format, merchant KYC and refund/consumer/commercial terms apply. The under-18 founder cannot bypass account or signatory eligibility checks.
- Keep sample invoices fictional in the synthetic test environment. Do not point a live bank/UPI personal QR at clients. Do not publish private merchant credentials or any customer tax documents into the public GitHub repository.

### Payment Phase P1 — first legitimately contracted B2B case

1. Authorized contracting party issues a **written quote** defining question, measurable deliverables, exclusions, independent expert, deadlines if realistically serviceable, case-volume cap, data rights, invoicing/tax terms, cancellation and dispute procedure.
2. Buyer approves in writing, including PO if its procurement process requires one.
3. Authorized business issues compliant invoice and payment instructions to the **business-owned settlement account**.
4. Customer uses a supported business-bank transfer such as NEFT/RTGS/IMPS, subject to their procurement and banking rules. Record bank reference and financial book reconciliation.
5. Start work on the agreed condition only; collect the final milestone payment according to contracted acceptance terms.
6. Never infer bank settlement from an uploaded screenshot alone. Authorized finance owner verifies ledger.
7. Track receivables and next lawful action; professional accountant/counsel handles taxes, collection disputes and refunds.

### Payment Phase P2 — optional regulated gateway

If buyers actually want card/UPI/payment-link convenience, a legally eligible authorized merchant may use a **third-party hosted service** such as Razorpay or Cashfree, subject to provider onboarding, merchant KYC, entity/bank eligibility and actual pricing. Do not claim the project has a merchant account. Add payment-link reference to a customer invoice, payment/provider event audit, settlement reconciliation and refunds. Hosted gateway, *not* custom card/token storage.

**Future webhook controls:** server-only secrets; provider signature verification over raw event payload; replay/idempotency keys; expected amount/currency/invoice match; status state machine (created → authorized → captured/settled/refunded/failed, as provider semantics permit); duplicates/late callbacks safe; manual exception queue; separate final settlement reconciliation; negative tests of wrong signature, amount, tenant and repeated notifications. Payment event != income until verified ledger settlement.

### Payment Phase P3 — justified recurring billing

Only after authenticated paying renewal behavior: agreed periodic invoice or merchant-authorized recurring mandate (depending on permitted product/provider/terms), pro-rata changes, dunning with contractual consent, cancellations/refunds, tax audit and finance approval. Do not impose automatic recurring payment simply to create SaaS metrics.

**Payment records (proposed, not DDL):** `commercial_accounts`, `service_agreements`, `quotes`, `billing_invoices`, `payment_requests`, `payment_events`, `settlement_reconciliations`, `credit_notes`, `refund_requests`, `collection_tasks`. Scope every record to authorized tenant and contract. Financial record access should be narrower than operational-case views. No payment gateway implementation is approved on #136.

## 6. Risk, fraud, accounting and privacy controls

- Separate **contracted / invoiced / received / settled / refunded** balances; handle failed payments, duplicate notifications and partial payments correctly. Cash collected, not a quote or entire customer's receivable, is GridMind revenue.
- Use documented taxable-service classification and accountant review of GST threshold, registration and invoice rules; **no invented GST exemption**. Keep PAN/GST/bank fields private.
- Non-confidential public contact; customer evidence only after formal authorization and real data security verification. Indian DPDP obligations require professional applicability/timeline review when personal data is processed.
- Subcontractor contracts must cover competence, independence, confidentiality, liability, data access, deliverables and conflict disclosure. Never self-certify or imply professional accreditation.
- Refund policy should be transparent in proposal and cover cancelled/no-start work, material breach, scope impossibility, disputed delivery and failed duplicated collections consistent with applicable law and client contract.
- Tax/merchant disputes and account verification should be handled by qualified adults/professionals; no age-limit workaround.

## 7. Contribution economics and capital discipline

**Per case:** collected net fee − reviewer/subcontractor fees − founder shadow labor (hours × realistic replacement rate) − personnel time − travel − payment/provider costs − rework/quality failure and allocated variable support = **contribution before fixed overhead**. Report taxes and period adjustments separately under actual accounting treatment.

**Fictional sensitivity only:** case billed ₹30,000; reviewer ₹8,000 and directly allocable other effort ₹10,000 → ₹12,000 contribution **before** fixed overhead, taxes, bad debt or uncounted risk. The same ₹30,000 case with ₹40,000 direct cost destroys value. No quote, paid pilot or customer willingness has been observed.

**Go-to-paid case gate:** buyer with concrete recent pain; cheaper incumbent alternative benchmark; real budget/paid next step; eligible formal signatory/business bank; qualified reviewer; permitted records; tested tenant/custody where applicable; delivery cost and margin floor; written scope/insurance/liability advice as appropriate.

**Go-to-recurring gate:** customer paid and collected; objective repeat problem; additional cycles require service even after initial resolution; specialist staffing and turnaround are sustainable; gross contribution positive after realistic labor; no unauthorized data reuse. No scale claim without several independent cases.

## 8. Channels, retention and relationship ownership

Primary early distribution is founder/adult-supported industrial introductions and relationships with legitimate qualified quality/production/energy/commercial professionals. The website is a credibility and discovery channel, not proof of traction. No scraped member lists, purchased unverifiable leads, cold spam, fake customer endorsements or fabricated performance guarantees.

Customer retention is earned via useful follow-up: visible unresolved risks, named action owners, verification status, and post-case rechecks. A “subscription” must fund actual delivered review/service; no data hostages or impossible promised SLAs.

## 9. Commercial proof questions before funding/building

What expensive, recent problem did the customer experience? Who controls its budget? How do internal staff/ERP/accountants/consultants already handle it? Which specific failure remains? What will the customer pay for better handling? Which minimum authorized records can be used? Who can independently review them? What costs and responsibilities would GridMind accept? What could disprove the value claim? When would it occur again?

**Record answers ONLY in the canonical [Buyer Discovery Sprint](../gridmind-buyer-discovery-sprint-2026-10-11.md).** Missing evidence remains missing. Do not launch billing or label the commercial workflow operational merely because this SOP exists.
