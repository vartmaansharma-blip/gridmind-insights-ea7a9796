# GridMind Repository Decision-Consistency Audit

**Audit date:** 2026-10-11  
**Scope:** Core strategy, decision authority, master plan, wedge scorecard, buyer discovery, market research, India segment shortlist, problem-failure analysis, creative direction, and interactive prototype notes.  
**Method:** Fetched and compared current files on `main`. This is a document-consistency audit, not a full code/security audit or browser test.

## Executive result

The repository was broadly aligned on the core rule: preserve the long-term industrial-intelligence ambition, but do not choose the first commercial wedge or build a public marketing site ahead of evidence. The newest research files reinforced rather than overturned that rule.

The main inconsistency was status and discoverability:
- The master plan's top metadata still framed the plan primarily as visual exploration and did not reflect the latest commercial-validation priority.
- The canonical Decision Constitution had not yet recorded the latest problem-failure analysis and manufacturing workflow stress test.
- The newest analysis documents were not surfaced in the README/documentation index.
- The interactive prototype is committed but has no recorded browser/device, keyboard, or reduced-motion verification; it must remain a prototype.
- The scorecard and discovery/research documents correctly state that the wedge is not validated and do not claim interviews, customer data, permissions, savings, pricing commitments, or paid pilots.

## Canonical decisions after reconciliation

1. **Long-term ambition:** GridMind remains a broad industrial intelligence and value-realization platform thesis. This is a destination, not a claim of current capability.
2. **Immediate priority:** validate one narrow, consequential industrial workflow with a real buyer before broadening product scope or public marketing.
3. **Leading hypothesis only:** a human-reviewed, source-linked incident investigation packet for one recurring production/quality incident, using customer-approved existing records. This is not a selected or validated wedge.
4. **Alternatives remain open:** operational-loss investigation, one-category procurement intelligence, and a narrow vertical workflow must be compared using evidence.
5. **Evidence boundary:** separate source-backed facts, calculations, hypotheses, counter-evidence, and unknowns. Do not claim autonomous root-cause diagnosis or guaranteed savings.
6. **Economics:** establish conservative incremental buyer value, existing recovery/alternatives, adoption burden, manual delivery labor, and margin. Do not impose an unsupported universal rupee threshold.
7. **Buyer access remains unresolved:** no qualified buyer, interview, customer record, willingness-to-pay signal, or paid commitment is verified in this audit.
8. **Website/design:** Clarity Engineered remains a creative direction for exploration only. No full public-site build or product claims should bypass product-truth and offer gates. Browser/device validation remains pending.
9. **Decision rule:** advance, narrow, change wedge, or stop based on evidence. If access, data rights, material value, trustworthiness, or delivery economics fail, record the blocker or reject the hypothesis.

## What this audit did not establish

- It did not inspect every source file or every application route.
- It did not run tests, inspect deployment state, or test the prototype in a browser.
- It did not validate security, factory isolation, production schema, or live persistence.
- It did not establish customer demand, pricing, ROI, or a winning market segment.

## Next action

Resolve buyer access and conduct one real problem-first discovery conversation. If no legitimate path to a manufacturing buyer exists, record that blocker and seek an appropriate introduction channel. Do not fill this gap with invented prospects or more speculative product development.
