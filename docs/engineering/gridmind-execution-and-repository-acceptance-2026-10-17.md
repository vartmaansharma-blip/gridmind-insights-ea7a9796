### Qualified engineering-method reviewer: required checks

1. Define the measurement boundary, pressure reference (gauge/absolute), flow reference conditions (actual/normalized, temperature/humidity), instrument calibration and timestamp/clock alignment. Canonical unit labels and declared context strings do not prove physical comparability. Zero/off-state readings are currently rejected; decide whether that is appropriate for the bounded demonstration.
2. Challenge baseline selection and coverage: at least three earlier baseline intervals, exact declared context, 0.2 bar pressure and 10% flow tolerances. These are unvalidated demonstration parameters. Independently reproduce representative positive, negative, sparse/missing-baseline, mixed-context, conflicting/overlapping-time and high-uncertainty fixtures; specify what additional fixtures/evidence are needed before approval.
3. Independently calculate kW/flow ratios, baseline envelopes and residual ranges, including uncertainty in both power and flow. The single declared percentage is treated as a bound, not measured calibration evidence; pressure uncertainty, covariance, drift, serial dependence and model inadequacy are not established. These ranges are **not statistical confidence intervals**.
4. Challenge first-comparable-before pairing, reuse of a before interval, unequal durations, unmatched regimes and selection bias. There is no time-weighted energy aggregation or attributable saving. Demonstrate that insufficient comparability remains inconclusive and that hypotheses never become reviewed engineering findings automatically.
5. Review source-line findings, uncertainty/counter-explanations, reviewer independence and the simulated approval/action/verification records. The imported synthetic after-period can predate the recorded action; it is **not a prospective intervention study**. A completed action is not proof of efficacy; causal outcome remains inconclusive and verified savings null.
