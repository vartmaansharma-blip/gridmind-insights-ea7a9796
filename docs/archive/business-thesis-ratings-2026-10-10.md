## Decision rule

Run a short discovery sprint across GridMind, one procurement category, and one specific vertical workflow. Score each on documented evidence, not founder enthusiasm. The winner must have:
- a named buyer and recent painful event;
- authorized data and a qualified reviewer;
- a measurable baseline and a useful deliverable;
- a credible willingness-to-pay signal or concrete purchasing next step;
- repeatability and viable delivery economics.

A fatal blocker (no data rights, no buyer, unsafe scope, no credible reviewer) overrides the numeric score. Re-rate after real interviews and evidence; until then, treat all scores as provisional.
