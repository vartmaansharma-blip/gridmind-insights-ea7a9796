# GridMind

**GridMind aims to become a globally relevant industrial and critical-infrastructure intelligence company, serving India and eventually international markets. Prove one valuable product before expanding.**

Start with one investigation: permissioned evidence → observations and competing hypotheses → qualified review → customer-approved action → verified or inconclusive outcome. Compressed air is the initial technical exemplar; **no number-one commercial opportunity is approved**. The premature Manufacturing Quality-Loss Intelligence ranking is withdrawn. Initial research covers B2B industrial metal-component contract manufacturing: stamping/pressing/fabrication, CNC machining, and casting/forging, investigated separately. Existing electricity development is retained.

## Repository and trust

`src/` contains the app, `supabase/` backend source, `tests/` and `scripts/` validation, `data/` fictional fixtures and `docs/` the operating record. [Security](SECURITY.md), [support](SUPPORT.md), [change history](CHANGELOG.md) and [ownership](.github/CODEOWNERS) retain their specific purposes.

The repository is public; no open-source license has been selected. Public visibility does not grant unrestricted reuse of original code. Keep credentials, customer records and private commercial/security information out of issues and history; use the security reporting route. Dated research, archived plans and `roadmap.md` are references, not extra execution queues.
